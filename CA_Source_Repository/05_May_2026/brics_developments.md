# Issue Dossier

## 1. What Happened
BRICS Tourism Ministers adopted the **Jaipur Declaration** by consensus at their ministerial meeting in Jaipur on 21 August 2026. The declaration identifies four priorities: use of artificial intelligence in tourism; sustainable and responsible tourism; skilling and capacity-building; and greater exchanges and seamless travel among BRICS countries.

The meeting used the theme **“Building for Resilience, Innovation, Cooperation and Sustainability.”** Ministers also identified cooperation in destination management, tourism data and statistics, investment and infrastructure, heritage, and people-to-people contact. Representatives of nine BRICS members participated.

On 22 August, the 14th BRICS Science, Technology and Innovation Ministerial Meeting in Chennai unanimously adopted the **Chennai Declaration**. It endorsed an effective, resilient and future-ready STI framework; the 2026-27 STI calendar; work on research infrastructure and mega-science; and further operationalisation of the **BRICS Global Research Advanced Infrastructure Network (BRICS GRAIN)**. Cooperation areas include AI, high-performance computing, quantum technologies, biotechnology, health, advanced materials and climate-related research.

On 23 August, BRICS Sports Ministers adopted the **BRICS Sports Joint Declaration** by consensus at Visakhapatnam. They advanced three practical initiatives: a BRICS Working Group on Sports Technology; a proposed BRICS Sports Manufacturing Conclave in Udaipur in November 2026; and proposed Traditional and Indigenous Sports Demonstration Games in Ahmedabad on 12-13 October 2026. The working group covers sports science, athlete performance, governance, artificial intelligence, digital transformation and innovation. The Jaipur, Chennai and Visakhapatnam texts are ministerial political commitments and cooperation frameworks; none was presented as a ratified treaty creating enforceable individual rights.

## 2. Background
BRIC began as an economic acronym for Brazil, Russia, India and China. The countries began formal political coordination and held their first summit in 2009. South Africa joined in 2010, turning BRIC into BRICS. The grouping later expanded to include additional major emerging economies. An official Indian 2026 BRICS release lists eleven members: Brazil, China, Egypt, Ethiopia, India, Indonesia, Iran, Russia, Saudi Arabia, South Africa and the United Arab Emirates.

BRICS has no constituent charter comparable to the UN Charter and no supranational authority that can legislate for members. It usually works by intergovernmental consultation and consensus. India holds the BRICS chairship in 2026 and has used sectoral ministerial meetings to advance practical cooperation under its broader priorities.

Sports cooperation predates the 2026 declaration: BRICS Sports Ministers have met under rotating chairships and a joint working group was recognised in earlier ministerial texts. The Visakhapatnam outcome adds a technology-focused work stream and proposed manufacturing and indigenous-sports events under India's chairship.

## 3. Timeline
- **2001**: Economist Jim O'Neill popularises the acronym BRIC for four large emerging economies.
- **2006**: BRIC foreign ministers begin formal political meetings.
- **2009**: The first BRIC leaders' summit is held in Yekaterinburg, Russia.
- **2010**: South Africa joins; the grouping becomes BRICS.
- **2014**: BRICS members sign the treaty establishing the New Development Bank.
- **1 January 2024**: Egypt, Ethiopia, Iran and the United Arab Emirates enter the expanded grouping.
- **January 2025**: Indonesia is announced as a full BRICS member.
- **2026**: India holds the BRICS chairship.
- **21 August 2026**: Tourism Ministers adopt the Jaipur Declaration by consensus.
- **22 August 2026**: Science and Technology Ministers unanimously adopt the Chennai Declaration and endorse the 2026-27 STI calendar.
- **23 August 2026**: Sports Ministers adopt the BRICS Sports Joint Declaration by consensus in Visakhapatnam and advance three cooperation initiatives.

## 4. Key People & Organisations
- **BRICS Tourism Ministers**: Adopted the Jaipur Declaration and set sectoral cooperation priorities.
- **Ministry of Tourism, Government of India**: Hosted and coordinated the Jaipur meeting during India's chairship.
- **BRICS Science and Technology Ministers**: Adopted the Chennai Declaration at the 14th ministerial meeting.
- **Department of Science and Technology**: Coordinated the Chennai meeting and BRICS STI work streams during India's chairship.
- **BRICS GRAIN**: Proposed network for shared access to advanced research infrastructure; it is not the similarly named BRICS Grain Exchange for agricultural commodities.
- **BRICS Sports Ministers**: Adopted the Visakhapatnam declaration and advanced technology, manufacturing and traditional-sports cooperation.
- **BRICS Working Group on Sports Technology**: Cooperation mechanism for sports science, performance, governance, AI and digital innovation; it is not an international regulator.
- **BRICS member governments**: Implement cooperation voluntarily through their national institutions and future working arrangements.
- **New Development Bank (NDB)**: A treaty-based development bank created by BRICS members; it is institutionally distinct from a ministerial declaration.
- **UN Tourism**: The United Nations specialised agency for responsible, sustainable and accessible tourism; it is not a BRICS organ.

## 5. Legal & Constitutional Significance
- The Jaipur, Chennai and Visakhapatnam declarations are **soft law/political cooperation**, not treaties shown as concluded and brought into force under international law. Their language can influence policy and future agreements without directly creating enforceable domestic rights.
- India's Union executive conducts foreign affairs under **Article 73** read with Parliament's fields in the Union List, including **Entry 10** (foreign affairs) and **Entry 14** (treaties and implementation).
- **Article 253** empowers Parliament to make laws for implementing treaties, agreements, conventions and decisions made at international conferences. A declaration does not itself amend Indian law.
- Executive participation in an international declaration cannot override a statute or fundamental right. Domestic regulatory changes still require the appropriate legal instrument.
- Consensus in BRICS means the participating governments reached a common political text; it is not the same as majority voting or judicial adjudication.
- AI-assisted tourism can raise domestic questions involving privacy, consumer protection, discrimination and data governance, even though the declaration does not decide those legal issues.
- Sports-technology cooperation can touch data protection, athlete welfare, anti-doping, intellectual property and sports-governance rules, but a ministerial declaration does not displace the competent domestic or international regulator.

## 6. Static GK Connection
- **BRICS** originally stood for Brazil, Russia, India, China and South Africa.
- The first summit was held in **Yekaterinburg, Russia, in 2009**.
- The **New Development Bank** is headquartered in **Shanghai, China** and began operations after the 2014 founding agreement.
- BRICS is an intergovernmental grouping, not a UN specialised agency, military alliance, customs union or free-trade area.
- **Jaipur** is the capital of Rajasthan and is known as the Pink City. Its walled city is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.
- Sustainable tourism seeks economic benefits while protecting communities, cultural heritage and the environment.
- **BRICS GRAIN** means BRICS Global Research Advanced Infrastructure Network and concerns shared scientific facilities.
- The **Young Scientist Forum**, **Young Innovator Prize** and multilateral calls under the BRICS STI Framework Programme are recurring cooperation mechanisms.
- **Visakhapatnam** is a major port city on the Bay of Bengal in Andhra Pradesh; **Udaipur** is in Rajasthan and **Ahmedabad** is in Gujarat.
- Traditional and indigenous sports can serve both cultural-preservation and sports-diplomacy goals.

## 7. CLAT Passage
At consecutive ministerial meetings in Jaipur, Chennai and Visakhapatnam, BRICS members adopted sectoral declarations by consensus. Jaipur focuses on tourism; Chennai on frontier science and shared research infrastructure; and Visakhapatnam on sports technology, manufacturing and traditional sports. These declarations can coordinate national policy and guide later cooperation, but they do not automatically operate as Indian statutes or ratified treaties. A proposed conclave or demonstration games remains different from a completed event, and a BRICS working group is not a supranational regulator. If implementation requires changing legal rights or spending public money, the competent Indian authorities must use the Constitution's ordinary legislative, executive and financial processes.

## 12. Confusion Traps
- **BRICS vs NDB**: BRICS is the political grouping; NDB is a separate bank created by treaty.
- **Declaration vs treaty**: Consensus language can guide policy without automatic domestic enforceability.
- **BRICS vs UN Tourism**: UN Tourism is a UN specialised agency; BRICS is not part of the UN system.
- **Original five vs expanded membership**: The acronym reflects the original five even after later expansion.
- **Nine participants vs eleven listed members**: Nine member representatives attended the tourism meeting; the Indian 2026 official member list contains eleven countries.
- **AI cooperation vs AI legislation**: Endorsing AI use in tourism, research or sports does not enact a national data or AI law.
- **BRICS GRAIN vs Grain Exchange**: GRAIN is a scientific-infrastructure network; the Grain Exchange is a separate agricultural trading proposal.
- **Working group vs regulator**: The Sports Technology Working Group coordinates members; it does not license athletes or replace WADA, national federations or domestic law.
- **Advanced vs completed**: The Udaipur conclave and Ahmedabad demonstration games were proposed for later in 2026; they had not occurred on 23 August.
